1. Expand `/en/custom-software` for European companies looking for lean premium software delivery.

## Content Rules

- Do not invent clients, revenue, testimonials, reviews or rankings.
- Every article must link to a service page and one relevant case where possible.
- Every service page must link back to cases and supporting articles.
- Articles should solve the searcher's decision problem, not repeat sales copy.
