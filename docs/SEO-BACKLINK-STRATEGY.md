# SEO Backlink Strategy

Backlinks should support credibility and commercial discovery. Avoid spam directories, PBNs, paid link blasts and fake reviews.

## High Priority

| Opportunity | URL | Why | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clutch | https://clutch.co/ | B2B buyer marketplace for service providers; strong commercial fit. | Build profile, add real services, collect legitimate reviews. |
| GoodFirms | https://www.goodfirms.co/ | B2B research and review platform for software and IT services. | Create company profile, verify details, add real portfolio. |
| Product Hunt | https://www.producthunt.com/ | Useful for GastroApp or standalone UnderStack products when launch-ready. | Real product page, screenshots, clear positioning. |
| GitHub | https://github.com/ | Authority and developer trust if UnderStack publishes open-source utilities or examples. | Public repos, clean README, link back naturally. |
| LinkedIn company page | https://www.linkedin.com/ | Brand entity, founder trust and distribution. | Complete profile, consistent CVR/name/site, regular posts. |

## Medium Priority

| Opportunity | URL | Why | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| city-map Aarhus | https://city-map.com/en/business-directory/region-aarhus-arhus-dk | Aarhus-oriented directory visibility. | Confirm listing eligibility and business details. |
| Business in Denmark | https://businessindenmark.virk.dk/ | Official Danish business gateway; not a normal backlink directory, but important entity/admin reference. | Company login and correct business data. |
| B2BMAP Denmark | https://b2bmap.com/denmark/companies | Denmark business directory with listing option. | Validate category quality before submission. |
| PRLog Denmark directory | https://biz.prlog.org/dk/ | Free business directory and press release option. | Use only factual company/product news. |
| Dev.to | https://dev.to/ | Technical authority if publishing useful engineering content. | Publish real technical posts, not thin SEO reposts. |
| Medium | https://medium.com/ | Distribution for founder/engineering insights. | Canonical back to UnderStack article where possible. |

## Low Priority / Validate First

| Opportunity | URL | Why | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ezilon Denmark Business | https://www.ezilon.com/regional/denmark/business_and_economy/index.shtml | General Denmark directory. | Check submission quality and category relevance. |
| Expat.com Denmark Business | https://www.expat.com/en/business/europe/denmark/ | May help international discovery. | Relevant only if services target expat/international businesses. |
| LocalBizNetwork Denmark | https://www.localbiznetwork.com/denmark | General business listing. | Validate spam level before use. |
| CompanyData Denmark | https://companydata.com/directory/denmark/ | Danish company directory/search. | Mostly citation/entity discovery rather than active lead gen. |

## Partnership Strategy

- Ask real clients for permission to publish case studies.
- Offer discreet optional "Developed by UnderStack" attribution on client sites only when voluntarily approved.
- Build restaurant-industry relationships around GastroApp content.
- Publish practical engineering posts and link to relevant UnderStack service pages.

Sources checked during research: Clutch, GoodFirms, Business in Denmark, city-map Aarhus, B2BMAP Denmark, PRLog Denmark, Ezilon Denmark, Expat.com Denmark, LocalBizNetwork Denmark and CompanyData Denmark.
